About BookCycle Works

Founded in Dayton, Ohio, BookCycle Works is on a mission to keep quality books in circulation. We rescue clean, gently used books from homes, estates, and collections and give them new life through resale channels.

We focus exclusively on books in almost new condition — no heavy library markings, minimal wear, recent publications preferred. The rest are responsibly recycled or donated to local charities.
